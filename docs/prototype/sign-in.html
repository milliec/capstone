I designed this tool to read fund regulations and draft a compliance calendar: who must file what, and by when. A wrong date has real consequences, so I started from one question: where can I trust AI, and where can't I?

My approach is: decide what must never go wrong, make code and people responsible for that, and let AI speed up everything else. Then scope ruthlessly to what can actually ship.
